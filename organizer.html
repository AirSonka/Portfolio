For each photo, set a Title, an optional short Caption (a sentence shown smaller, under the title), and one or more Series (short group names — e.g. "Coastal", "Portraits", "Night"). A photo can belong to multiple series at once — just separate them with a comma, e.g. "Coastal, Selected Photos". Photos sharing a series get grouped together on the site. Leave Series blank to leave a photo in "Uncategorized" for now, and leave Caption blank if you just want a title.

When you're done, click "Download finished photos-data.js" and move the downloaded file into your site's js/ folder, replacing the old one.

To also add a short intro sentence under each series heading on the site, edit js/series-data.js directly — it's a short file, easy to hand-edit, listing one line per series.

Your typing is auto-saved in this browser tab via "Save progress", so you can close this page and come back later without losing work — as long as you use the same browser on the same computer.
